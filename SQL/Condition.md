Where Clause is use for filtering 

= **Select \* From table\_name WHERE Condition ;**



with AND Operate Example 

* **Select \* From table\_name WHERE AGE > 30 AND COUNTRY = 'INDIA';**



**ORDER BY** 

* **Select \* From table\_name WHERE AGE > 30 AND COUNTRY = 'INDIA** **ORDER BY AMOUNT DEC;**
